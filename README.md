# 🛒 Olist E-Commerce — Sales Funnel & RFM Analysis

> **Portfolio Project** | Data Analyst | Python · SQL · Power BI / Tableau

---

## Business Problem

Olist is a Brazilian e-commerce marketplace connecting small retailers to major marketplaces. With 100k+ orders across 2 years, the business needs to understand **where customers drop off in the purchase funnel** and **which customer segments drive the most revenue** — so marketing and retention budgets can be allocated effectively.

## Hypothesis

> A minority of customers (≈20%) generate the majority of revenue, and they share identifiable purchasing patterns that can be replicated across the broader base.

---

## Project Goals

| # | Goal | Output |
|---|------|--------|
| 1 | Map the full purchase funnel and identify drop-off points | Funnel chart |
